⬇ Get the app
Cartoon-style survival with loud sounds. An original game, not connected to any other game.

How to play

The goal

Survive 99 nights. The game saves every morning, so you can come back and continue.

The campfire

It is your safe place. Wolves will not come close while it burns. Press E at the fire to add logs. If it goes out at night, you are in big trouble. Upgrade it at the crafting bench to burn longer and protect a bigger area. Follow the orange arrow to find camp.

Gather

Click trees with your axe for logs. Press E on berry bushes, scrap piles and chests. Hunt deer and bunnies for meat, then cook it at the fire. Press F to eat.

Craft

At the bench next to the fire make a better axe, a spear, bullets, a rifle, medkits and campfire upgrades.

Play with friends

Tap Play with friends, host a game and share the 4-letter room code. Up to 4 players survive together online: shared world, shared campfire, shared night. If you go down you wake up at dawn. If everyone goes down, it is game over.

Rescue the kids

Five lost children are hiding in camps around the forest. Find them and press E to bring them back to camp. Each one gives you a reward.

Danger

Wolves, huge alpha wolves and angry bears come out at night. Fight with your axe, spear or rifle, stay near the fire and keep your hunger up.
